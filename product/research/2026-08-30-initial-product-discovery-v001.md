# Initial product discovery — v001

Verified: 2026-08-30

## Product direction

Bootcamper should combine self-paced interactive lessons with human mentoring and applied feedback. Admins and mentors author structured lessons. Learners consume them as presentations or web lessons, answer questions, submit practical work, and retain progress against an immutable lesson version.

## Evidence

- [Canvas Basics Guide](https://community.canvaslms.com/html/assets/Canvas_Basics_Guide.pdf): learning platforms organize content into sequenced modules, prerequisites, requirements, and visible progress.
- [Moodle File resource](https://docs.moodle.org/310/en/File_resource): established learning platforms treat authored and uploaded learning material as first-class course resources.
- [Microsoft PowerPoint accessibility guidance](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/make-your-powerpoint-presentations-accessible-to-people-with-disabilities-6f7772b2-2f33-4bd2-8ca7-dae3b2b3ef25): presentation accessibility depends on structure, titles, reading order, alternative text, contrast, captions, and accessible link text.
- [Coursera Drivers of Quality](https://about.coursera.org/press/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Coursera_DriversOfQuality_Book_MCR-1126-V4-lr.pdf): goals, schedules, progress accountability, and social participation are associated with learner engagement and completion.
- [MentorCruise mentor expectations](https://help.mentorcruise.com/article/24-expectations): useful mentoring includes goal support, actionable feedback, work review, exercises, and continuity.
- [MENTOR effective practice](https://mentoring.org/blog/research/elements-leads-effective-practice/): effective mentoring programs include recruitment, screening, training, matching, monitoring, support, and deliberate closure.
- [MENTOR relationship quality](https://mentoring.org/resource/getting-started-with-program-evaluation-3-measuring-relationship-quality/): compatibility, trust, growth orientation, support frequency, and program structure affect relationship quality.
- [GitHub Education Classroom Report](https://education.github.com/classroom-report/2018): applied workflows were associated with stronger perceived portfolio, collaboration, project-management, and career preparation.

## Human decisions

- Admins and mentors author lessons.
- Lessons are structured block-based presentations, not free-form slide canvases.
- MVP learning is self-paced.
- Closed questions may be checked automatically; mentors review open answers and practical work.
- Content hierarchy is Program → Module → Interactive Lesson → Slides and learning blocks.
- Published lessons are immutable; changes create a new version with a reason.
- Live shared lectures remain a required post-MVP direction.
