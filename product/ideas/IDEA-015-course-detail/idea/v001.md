# Course detail page

## Product problem

A catalog summary cannot provide enough context for a learner to make an informed enrollment decision.

## Intended outcome

A visitor sees a course's purpose, audience, outcomes, structure, prerequisites, expected commitment, availability, and mentoring model. The page explains enrollment state and next action, recognizes already enrolled learners, and avoids dead-end actions for unavailable courses.

## Evidence

- [Blackboard course catalog behavior](https://help.blackboard.com/Learn/Administrator/SaaS/User_Interface_Options/Ultra_Experience/Base_Navigation/Courses), verified 2026-08-30, separates catalog browsing, opening a course, and self-enrolling.
- [Blackboard course management](https://help.blackboard.com/Platform_Extensions/Training_And_Development_Manager/Administrator/Course_Manager/Course_Management), verified 2026-08-30, identifies availability, categories, prerequisites, instructors, and offerings as distinct information.

## Scores

- User benefit: 5/5
- Progressiveness: 3/5
- Business value: 5/5
- Confidence: 5/5

## Priority rationale

This is required between catalog discovery and self-enrollment and remains independently reviewable from both.
