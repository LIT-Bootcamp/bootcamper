# Collaborative and advanced lesson authoring

## Product problem

As the content library matures, authors may need review collaboration, richer visual composition, reusable templates, and assistance maintaining lesson quality at scale.

## Intended outcome

Admins and mentors collaborate on lesson drafts, review proposed changes, reuse proven structures, and create richer presentation experiences without losing accessibility, version history, or learner progress integrity.

Potential future outcomes include collaborative review, reusable block templates, a more flexible canvas, and assisted authoring. They are not MVP requirements.

## Evidence

- Block-oriented authoring supports extensible learning structures, while human decisions intentionally defer free-form slide composition.
- The need for collaborative or assisted authoring should be demonstrated by real author behavior after launch.

## Scores

- User benefit: 3/5
- Progressiveness: 5/5
- Business value: 4/5
- Confidence: 3/5

## Priority rationale

Keep this as a post-MVP direction. Building a PowerPoint competitor or collaborative editor before validating structured lesson creation would delay the central learner outcome.
