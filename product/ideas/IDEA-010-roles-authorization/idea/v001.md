# Roles and authorization

## Product problem

Learners, mentors, and admins have different responsibilities, but the product lacks explicit business rules defining who may see and change what.

## Intended outcome

Every protected product action has an understandable responsible role. Learners access their own learning, mentors access only granted content and learner responsibilities, admins perform platform-wide operations, unauthorized users are refused consistently, and role changes preserve prior records.

## Evidence

- [Moodle role assignment guidance](https://docs.moodle.org/39/en/admin/tool/cohortroles/index), verified 2026-08-30, demonstrates that permissions depend on both role and context.
- [Blackboard Course Catalog configuration](https://help.blackboard.com/Platform_Extensions/Course_Catalog/Administrator/Configuration/TDMV2), verified 2026-08-30, separates administrative, course, instructor, learner, reporting, and progress responsibilities.

## Scores

- User benefit: 5/5
- Progressiveness: 3/5
- Business value: 5/5
- Confidence: 5/5

## Priority rationale

Every admin, authoring, mentoring, and learner flow depends on clear authority. Analyze alongside account access.
