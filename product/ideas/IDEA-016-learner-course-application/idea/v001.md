# Learner course selection and application

## Product problem

A learner can identify a suitable course but cannot express informed interest, provide course-specific context, or understand whether they have been admitted.

## Intended outcome

An eligible learner selects a course accepting applications, reviews its expectations, planned start date, and admission process, then answers required or optional text, single-choice, and multiple-choice questions. The learner reviews and submits one valid application, sees a pending state, and later sees acceptance or rejection with the admin's explanation. An accepted learner sees whether the course is waiting or active. Defining questions, reviewing applicants, deciding admission, sending notifications, and activating the cohort belong to `IDEA-018`.

## Evidence

- [Moodle request and approval guidance](https://docs.moodle.org/26/en/ELIS_Class_request_and_approval), verified 2026-08-30, demonstrates configurable request questions followed by approve or deny review.
- [Canvas Catalog Guide](https://community.canvaslms.com/html/assets/Canvas_Catalog_Guide.pdf), verified 2026-08-30, supports account creation in the context of catalog registration.
- Human product decision on 2026-08-30: learners choose courses and submit course-specific answers for admin selection.
