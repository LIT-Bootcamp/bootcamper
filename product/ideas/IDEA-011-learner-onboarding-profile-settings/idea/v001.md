# Learner onboarding, profile, and settings

## Product problem

A new learner has an account, but Bootcamper does not know enough about their goals, experience, preferences, or circumstances to guide learning and mentoring.

## Intended outcome

A learner completes short onboarding, records goals and relevant context, later updates allowed profile information, and controls relevant language, time-zone, privacy, and communication preferences. Optional information remains clearly optional and its purpose is visible.

## Evidence

- [Canvas learner profile guidance](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Gu%C3%ADa-del-Estudiante/C%C3%B3mo-edito-mi-perfil-en-mi-cuenta-de-usuario/ta-p/442769), verified 2026-08-30, distinguishes identity, biography, contact choices, and links.
- Existing `IDEA-005` and `IDEA-006` establish that goals and context affect guided journeys and mentor fit.

## Scores

- User benefit: 4/5
- Progressiveness: 3/5
- Business value: 4/5
- Confidence: 5/5

## Priority rationale

This follows account ownership and role boundaries; it should not make initial registration unnecessarily long.
