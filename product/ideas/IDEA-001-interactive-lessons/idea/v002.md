# Lesson and presentation authoring

## Product problem

Authorized authors need to create and maintain presentation-based learning material without mixing authoring responsibilities with learner enrollment or course consumption.

## Intended outcome

Admins and authorized mentors create, view, edit, preview, publish, archive, and safely remove lesson drafts. Lessons contain ordered presentation slides and structured learning blocks. Authors can understand publication state and previous published versions, while published learner history remains meaningful after later changes. Learner delivery belongs to `IDEA-017`; advanced collaborative authoring remains in `IDEA-008`.
