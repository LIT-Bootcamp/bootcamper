# Interactive lesson authoring and delivery

## Product problem

Bootcamper has no primary learning experience. Static files or external slide decks cannot combine accessible presentation, learning interaction, consistent navigation, and durable progress inside the mentoring platform.

## Intended outcome

Admins and mentors create structured interactive lessons made of slides and reusable learning blocks. Learners can consume the same lesson in focused presentation mode or continuous web-lesson mode, resume from their last position, and understand what is expected next.

The outcome includes text, images, code, links or embedded material, questions, practical tasks, learner-visible guidance, mentor-only notes, preview, draft publication, archive, and immutable published versions. It does not require a free-form PowerPoint-like canvas.

## Evidence

- Canvas and Moodle treat structured modules and learning resources as core platform capabilities; verified 2026-08-30.
- Microsoft accessibility guidance shows why presentation structure and accessible content are necessary rather than optional polish; verified 2026-08-30.
- Human product decisions recorded in `product/research/2026-08-30-initial-product-discovery-v001.md`.

## Scores

- User benefit: 5/5
- Progressiveness: 5/5
- Business value: 5/5
- Confidence: 5/5

## Priority rationale

This is the first product foundation: without an authored learning experience Bootcamper is only generic mentoring infrastructure. It should be analyzed before downstream programs, assessment, progress, or live delivery.
