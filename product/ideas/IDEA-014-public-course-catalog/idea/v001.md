# Public course catalog

## Product problem

Prospective learners cannot understand what Bootcamper offers or find a suitable course before committing to an account or enrollment.

## Intended outcome

Visitors and learners browse available published courses, compare concise information, narrow the catalog as it grows, and open a dedicated course detail page. Draft, archived, unavailable, and private courses do not appear publicly.

## Evidence

- [Blackboard Courses and Course Catalog](https://help.blackboard.com/Learn/Administrator/SaaS/User_Interface_Options/Ultra_Experience/Base_Navigation/Courses), verified 2026-08-30, distinguishes public browsing, details, self-enrollment, and enrolled-user courses.
- [Canvas Catalog Guide](https://community.canvaslms.com/html/assets/Canvas_Catalog_Guide.pdf), verified 2026-08-30, treats listings, registration, enrollments, and learner courses as separate journeys.

## Scores

- User benefit: 5/5
- Progressiveness: 3/5
- Business value: 5/5
- Confidence: 5/5

## Priority rationale

This is the acquisition and discovery surface once at least one course can be published.
