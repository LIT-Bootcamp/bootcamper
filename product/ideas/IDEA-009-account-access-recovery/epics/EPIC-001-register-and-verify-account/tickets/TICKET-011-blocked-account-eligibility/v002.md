# TICKET-011: Establish blocked-account sign-in eligibility

## Observable goal

A persisted blocked account can confirm email ownership without becoming eligible for ordinary sign-in; blocked credentials receive the existing generic sign-in rejection and create no session.

## Source IDEA, EPIC, version, and scenario IDs

IDEA-009/v003; EPIC-001/v002; SCENARIO-012.

## Acceptance evidence

Focused model and request specs prove that a blocked, unconfirmed learner can be confirmed without clearing the blocked state, then cannot sign in with correct credentials and receives no authenticated session. The rejected response matches the generic invalid-credentials outcome.

## Dependencies

none

## Difficulty (1-5)

3

## Estimate (ideal days, at most 2)

1

## Risks

An authentication override could accidentally bypass Devise Confirmable or expose a blocked state through a distinct failure message. Existing-session invalidation is intentionally outside this ticket and remains TICKET-004's responsibility.

## External actions

The administrator-owned blocking system must use the agreed persisted block/unblock transition; no administration interface is included here.

## Allowed scope

The `users` blocked-state migration, `User` block predicate and Devise sign-in eligibility hook, generic failure mapping, and focused model/request specs. No confirmation-proof lifecycle changes, block/unblock UI, existing-session invalidation, or recovery behavior.
