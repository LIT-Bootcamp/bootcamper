# Account access and recovery

## Product problem

People cannot reliably enter or return to Bootcamper without self-service registration, authentication, and account recovery.

## Intended outcome

A visitor creates a learner account; an existing user signs in and signs out; ownership is verified where required; forgotten credentials can be recovered safely; and blocked or inactive accounts cannot use ordinary access flows. Failures explain the next action without revealing another person's account.

## Business analysis outcome

The approved outcome is completely described by three independently readable EPICs:

- `EPIC-001` — register and verify a learner account;
- `EPIC-002` — start and end an authenticated session;
- `EPIC-003` — recover account access.

Together they define 13 requirements and 34 happy-path, edge, and error scenarios. Two clarification rounds resolved account identifiers, minimum registration data, confirmation gating, restricted-account behavior, account privacy, and session invalidation after recovery.

## Evidence

- [Moodle login settings](https://docs.moodle.org/502/en/Login_settings), verified 2026-08-30, treats self-registration, login, guest access, and password recovery as distinct policies.
- [Moodle email self-registration](https://docs.moodle.org/en/Email-based_self-registration), verified 2026-08-30, covers account creation, email confirmation, and confirmation resend.
