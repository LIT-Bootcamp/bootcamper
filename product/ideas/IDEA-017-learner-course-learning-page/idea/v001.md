# Learner course and learning page

## Product problem

After enrollment, learners need one coherent place to understand their active course, find the next lesson, resume learning, and see course-specific progress.

## Intended outcome

A learner sees enrolled courses and opens their structure and status. An accepted learner waiting for launch sees the planned start date and cannot consume learning material. When the planned date arrives or an admin chooses `Start now`, the course becomes active and exposes the next lesson or action. The learner consumes presentation lessons and resumes from the previous position. Locked, incomplete, submitted, blocked, and completed states are understandable; mentor context and feedback remain reachable. Admissions decisions and activation policy belong to `IDEA-018`.

## Evidence

- [Blackboard Courses](https://help.blackboard.com/Learn/Administrator/SaaS/User_Interface_Options/Ultra_Experience/Base_Navigation/Courses), verified 2026-08-30, describes an enrolled-user course page distinct from the public catalog.
- [Canvas Basics Guide](https://community.canvaslms.com/html/assets/Canvas_Basics_Guide.pdf), verified 2026-08-30, uses modules to sequence activities, enforce requirements, and expose progress.
- Existing `IDEA-001`, `IDEA-003`, and `IDEA-004` establish delivery, assessment, and progress outcomes presented here.
