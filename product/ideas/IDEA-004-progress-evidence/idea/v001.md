# Learning progress and evidence

## Product problem

Learners and mentors cannot manage momentum when they cannot distinguish opened content, completed learning, submitted work, blocked progress, and mentor-approved evidence.

## Intended outcome

Learners and mentors see understandable progress across lessons, modules, and programs. Progress distinguishes consumption, responses, submissions, revision, and approval. Learners can resume work and mentors can identify where support is needed.

## Evidence

- Canvas exposes progression requirements and module progress; verified 2026-08-30.
- Coursera research associates progress accountability with engagement and completion; verified 2026-08-30.

## Scores

- User benefit: 5/5
- Progressiveness: 5/5
- Business value: 5/5
- Confidence: 4/5

## Priority rationale

Progress is foundational but depends on the meaning of lessons, assignments, and review. Define it alongside those flows and deliver it after their observable states are known.
