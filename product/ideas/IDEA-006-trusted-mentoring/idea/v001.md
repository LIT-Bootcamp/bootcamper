# Trusted mentor participation and fit

## Product problem

Subject expertise alone does not create a safe or effective mentoring relationship. Learners need an appropriate mentor, while mentors need clear expectations, boundaries, learning context, and support.

## Intended outcome

Learners understand mentor expertise, availability, approach, and trust signals. Both sides assess fit against the learner's goal and program before accepting. Mentors receive clear responsibilities, boundaries, preparation, and ongoing support; learners can report concerns.

## Evidence

- MENTOR identifies recruitment, screening, training, matching, monitoring, support, and closure as effective-practice foundations; verified 2026-08-30.
- MENTOR relationship-quality guidance highlights compatibility, trust, satisfaction, and support structure; verified 2026-08-30.

## Scores

## Priority rationale
