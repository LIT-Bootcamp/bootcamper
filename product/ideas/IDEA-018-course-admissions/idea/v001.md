# Course admissions and cohort activation

## Product problem

Admins cannot collect course-specific applicant information, select suitable students, communicate decisions responsibly, or control when an accepted cohort begins learning.

## Intended outcome

For each application-based course, an authorized admin adds, views, changes, orders, and removes application questions with text, single-choice, or multiple-choice answers and required or optional status. The admin sees applicants and their answers, accepts selected students, or rejects an application with a learner-facing explanation while retaining who made the decision.

Accepted applicants receive an acceptance email; rejected applicants receive an email containing the explanation. Both decisions remain visible inside Bootcamper. Accepted learners enter a waiting course state until its planned start date, while pending and rejected applicants have no learning access. The course opens automatically on that date or earlier when an authorized admin chooses `Start now`, without erasing admission history.

## Evidence

- [Blackboard Course Catalog release notes](https://help.blackboard.com/Platform_Extensions/Release_Notes), verified 2026-08-30, document manager-controlled enrollment, review, learner status updates, enrollment email, course dates, and controlled access.
- [Canvas Catalog Guide](https://community.canvaslms.com/html/assets/Canvas_Catalog_Guide.pdf), verified 2026-08-30, documents learner email for account, enrollment, waitlist, and course lifecycle events.
- [Moodle request and approval guidance](https://docs.moodle.org/26/en/ELIS_Class_request_and_approval), verified 2026-08-30, demonstrates configurable request questions followed by authorized approve or deny review.
- Human product decision on 2026-08-30 defines admin selection, decision explanations, email, waiting, scheduled start, and `Start now`.
