# Live shared lectures

## Product problem

Self-paced lessons do not provide the energy, immediacy, shared questions, and group guidance of a mentor-led session.

## Intended outcome

A mentor starts a live lecture from an existing interactive lesson, participants join, and the mentor guides the shared slide position while allowing questions and learning interactions. After the session, the lesson and individual progress remain available for self-paced continuation.

## Evidence

- Coursera research associates social participation with learning engagement; verified 2026-08-30.
- Human product decision explicitly preserves shared lectures as a post-MVP direction.

## Scores

- User benefit: 4/5
- Progressiveness: 5/5
- Business value: 4/5
- Confidence: 3/5

## Priority rationale

Preserve this direction now, but defer it until self-paced content, interaction states, and real learner demand establish what must be synchronized.
