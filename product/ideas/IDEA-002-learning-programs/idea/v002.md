# Course and program authoring

## Product problem

Individual lessons need a managed course structure, but course administration should not be conflated with how learners discover, select, or consume courses.

## Intended outcome

Authorized authors create, view, update, publish, archive, and safely remove courses or programs. They define title, description, intended audience, outcomes, prerequisites, visibility, availability, application window, capacity, and planned start date; organize lessons into ordered modules; and understand which course version is published. Public discovery and course presentation belong to `IDEA-014` and `IDEA-015`; applications and admissions belong to `IDEA-016` and `IDEA-018`; learner delivery belongs to `IDEA-017`.

## Evidence

- [Canvas Basics Guide](https://community.canvaslms.com/html/assets/Canvas_Basics_Guide.pdf), verified 2026-08-30, documents modules, sequencing, prerequisites, requirements, and progress.
- [Blackboard course management](https://help.blackboard.com/Platform_Extensions/Training_And_Development_Manager/Administrator/Course_Manager/Course_Management), verified 2026-08-30, separates course availability, categories, prerequisites, instructors, and offerings.
