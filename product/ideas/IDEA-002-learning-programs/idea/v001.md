# Learning programs and assignments

## Product problem

Individual lessons do not create a coherent journey. Learners need an understandable sequence, while admins and mentors need to reuse and assign content without rebuilding a plan for every person.

## Intended outcome

Admins and mentors organize interactive lessons into programs and ordered modules, describe intended outcomes and prerequisites, and assign a program, module, or lesson to eligible learners or groups. Learners see their active path and next learning activity.

## Evidence

- Canvas documents modules, sequencing, prerequisites, requirements, and progress as core learning organization; verified 2026-08-30.
- Coursera research associates goals, schedules, and progress accountability with engagement; verified 2026-08-30.

## Scores

- User benefit: 5/5
- Progressiveness: 4/5
- Business value: 5/5
- Confidence: 5/5

## Priority rationale

This is the second foundation. It becomes valuable once the first interactive lesson lifecycle is defined and prevents Bootcamper from becoming an unstructured content repository.
