# Admin workspace

## Product problem

Authorized administrators need a clear operational entry point; otherwise administrative capabilities become disconnected pages that are difficult to discover and govern.

## Intended outcome

Admins enter a clearly identified workspace, navigate to separately approved user, role, course, lesson, enrollment, and oversight areas, and see operational states requiring attention. Learners and mentors cannot enter merely by knowing its location.

## Evidence

- [Blackboard course administration](https://help.blackboard.com/Learn/Administrator/SaaS/User_Interface_Options/Ultra_Experience/Base_Navigation/Courses), verified 2026-08-30, centralizes privileged course management through an administrator panel.
- [Blackboard Course Catalog configuration](https://help.blackboard.com/Platform_Extensions/Course_Catalog/Administrator/Configuration/TDMV2), verified 2026-08-30, exposes areas according to operational roles.

## Scores

- User benefit: 3/5
- Progressiveness: 2/5
- Business value: 5/5
- Confidence: 5/5

## Priority rationale

This is the administrative shell, not the CRUD itself. It should grow only when a separately approved capability exists.
