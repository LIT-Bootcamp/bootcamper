# Assessment and mentor feedback

## Product problem

Viewing slides or marking a lesson complete does not demonstrate understanding. Learners need actionable feedback, and mentors need a consistent way to review work without losing its learning context.

## Intended outcome

Lessons contain closed questions, open questions, and practical assignments. Closed answers can receive immediate results. Learners submit open or practical work for mentor review, receive approval or a change request, revise, and retain the feedback history against the lesson version they completed.

## Evidence

- MentorCruise describes exercises, actionable feedback, and work review as core mentoring value; verified 2026-08-30.
- GitHub Education reports stronger perceived career and portfolio preparation around applied work; verified 2026-08-30.

## Scores

- User benefit: 5/5
- Progressiveness: 5/5
- Business value: 5/5
- Confidence: 4/5

## Priority rationale

This completes the first meaningful learning loop. It follows basic lesson delivery but should shape lesson scenarios early so assessment is not bolted on after content publication.
